Frequently asked questions

How do I book a space?

Choose a space on the Our spaces page and tap Book inspection. This opens WhatsApp with a message already written, so you only need to send it. Our team will reply, confirm that the space is still available and agree an inspection day with you.

How much is the inspection fee and what does it cover?

The inspection fee is a flat ₦10,000. It covers your inspection visit, where you see the room, the compound and the facilities in person with one of our team, so you can decide with confidence.

Can I choose my own inspection day?

Yes. Inspection days are flexible. Tell us the day and time that suit you when you message us, and we will do our best to arrange it around your lectures.

Are the hostels and apartments really verified?

Yes. Every space is checked by our team before it is published on this site. We look at safety, water and power, cleanliness, the distance to campus and whether the price is fair for the area.

What types of rooms do you offer?

We list hostel rooms (2-man and 4-man rooms) and apartments such as self-contained rooms and flats. Use the budget and type filters on the Our spaces page to see only what fits you.

What does the price on each card cover?

The price shown is the rent for one session for the room type on the card. Each space is different, so ask us on WhatsApp what is included, for example utilities or caretaker charges, before you pay.

What does Sold Out mean?

Sold Out means every bed in that space has been taken for the current session. It stays visible so you can see what was available. Message us and we will suggest a similar space in your budget.

Can I watch videos of a space before visiting?

Yes. Most spaces have several photos and some have videos. Tap View all on any card to open the full gallery with every photo and video.

How do I pay once I have chosen a space?

After your inspection, our team will explain the payment steps and amounts and guide you until you are settled in. Please only pay through the details our team gives you, and ask us if you are unsure.

What if I cannot find a space in my budget?

Message us your budget, your preferred area and the room type you want. We will check the spaces we are adding and suggest the closest options for you.
